Leonida Legend — the best guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. Every trophy with its official name, description and grade, a guide in your own words that cites its sources, a spoiler-safe checklist saved in the browser, export and import, filters by kind and grade, a roadmap to the platinum, and the missable trophies flagged.
