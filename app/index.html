    How this guide works

    • Offline. Everything on this page comes from its own files — no network requests, no account, nothing to install. Your ticks are saved in this browser (localStorage) and can be exported to a file and imported back.
    • One missable trophy. Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy you can miss: do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — there is no mission replay.
    • No online trophies. All 55 unlock in single-player. There is no difficulty trophy and no mission medal to chase.
    • Sources. Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official list as published on PlayStation and tracked by Exophase and PSNProfiles. Each guide is written in our own words and names the guide it draws on, with a link where one exists. Five trophies were still unconfirmed when the game launched; their guides say so instead of guessing.
    • Artwork. This guide uses no Rockstar logo, official artwork, box art or game screenshots — the look is its own.
